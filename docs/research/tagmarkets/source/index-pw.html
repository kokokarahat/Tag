Regulatory Information

TAG Markets is a financial services brand operating through regulated entities in multiple jurisdictions.

T.M. Financials Ltd, incorporated in Mauritius (Company No. C185265), is regulated by the Financial Services Commission (FSC) Mauritius as an Investment Dealer (Licence No. GB21026474).

TM FINANCIALS SA (PTY) LTD, incorporated in South Africa (Registration No. 2024/508189/07), is an authorised Financial Services Provider (FSP No. 55237), regulated by the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA).

All clients registering through TAG Markets are onboarded with T.M. Financials Ltd (Mauritius), which is the contractual counterparty responsible for maintaining trading accounts and providing trading services. The South African entity provides marketing services only and does not operate client trading accounts.

Clients are subject to the regulatory framework and protections applicable to T.M. Financials Ltd under FSC Mauritius, rather than those applicable to the South African entity.

Risk Warning & Disclosure

Contracts for Difference (CFDs), leveraged foreign exchange, and other financial instruments involve significant risks and may not be suitable for all investors. Leverage can magnify both potential gains and losses, and you may lose your entire invested capital or more, depending on the applicable account terms. Before trading, carefully assess your financial circumstances, investment objectives, trading experience, and risk tolerance. Ensure you fully understand the risks involved, seek independent financial advice where necessary, and never trade with funds you cannot afford to lose. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Jurisdictional Restrictions & Investor Protection

TAG Markets does not accept clients residing in Spain or the United States. Services are offered internationally, subject to applicable laws, regulatory requirements, and T.M. Financials Ltd.'s FSC Mauritius license conditions. Client eligibility is assessed based on applicable jurisdictional restrictions, and TAG Markets reserves the right to decline or restrict services where necessary.

TAG Markets is not authorized or regulated by any European Union financial regulatory authority. Clients of T.M. Financials Ltd. are subject to the Mauritius regulatory framework and should not assume they benefit from EU investor compensation schemes or protections applicable to EU-authorized firms. Clients are encouraged to understand the applicable regulatory protections before trading.
